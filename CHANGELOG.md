## [0.1.0] – 2026-08-09

### Added
- Initial landing page with all five app cards (SHROUD, DRIFT, TRADE, PULSE, BLOOM, SECRET).
- Interactive overlays: FAQ, Build‑guide, Download, PayPal subscription.
- Access gates: ZIP (BLOOM), daily puzzle (DRIFT), age verification (PULSE), embed clicks (TRADE).
- Glitch‑themed visuals: particles, scanlines, vignette, neon text.

### Known Issues
- macOS, Linux, iOS links are placeholders – only Windows and Android APKs are available.
- SHROUD and SECRET are not yet downloadable.
