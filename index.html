// FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Why do you hate mainstream social media? ▼
Because it's a digital prison designed to silence dissent. I'm tired of the censorship, the algorithmic manipulation, and the constant data mining. The "Anti Social Network" is a rebellion against these echo chambers.
Why do you keep getting banned? ▼
The algorithms are built to enforce conformity, not freedom. I've been banned for sharing opinions that don't fit the mainstream narrative. This platform exists because I refuse to conform to arbitrary, ever‑changing rules.
What is the "Anti Social Network" exactly? ▼
It's a middle finger to the system. A collection of apps (Shroud, Trade, Pulse, Bloom, and the offline Drift) built for people who are tired of the sanitized, boring social media wasteland. It's a space where freedom of speech is paramount.
Are these apps actually real? ▼
Absolutely. Trade, Pulse, and Bloom are live and actively developed. Drift is currently an offline experiment representing the ultimate break from central authority. The browser links above will take you directly to the live web versions.
// BUILD YOUR OWN SOCIAL MEDIA

1. Choose Your Stack

Pick a backend that scales. Node.js + Express is a solid start. For real-time features, consider WebSockets or Socket.io. Database? PostgreSQL for structure, or MongoDB for flexibility.

2. Design the Data Model

Users, posts, comments, likes, follows — keep it simple at first. Think about privacy: encryption at rest, hashed passwords, and API keys that actually expire.

3. Build the API

REST is fine. GraphQL gives you more control. Just make sure your endpoints are secure: rate limiting, CORS, and JWT authentication are non-negotiable.

4. Create the Frontend

React, Vue, or Svelte — pick your poison. Keep it fast, keep it dark. Users love a good dark mode. And please, no infinite scroll just to sell ads.

5. Deploy & Scale

Dockerize everything. Use a reverse proxy (NGINX) and a CDN for static assets. Monitor with Prometheus + Grafana. And always, always have a kill switch.

6. Stay Independent

Don't rely on VC funding. Don't sell user data. Don't bow to censorship. Build something that outlasts the algorithms.
